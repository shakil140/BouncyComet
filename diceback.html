How to play

Three rules. That is the whole game.

No tutorial to sit through and no glossary to memorize. The How To Play screen fits on one page because the game fits on one page. You will understand DiceBack before your first roll lands.

  1. 1

    Roll the dice

    Tap to roll and get a number.

  2. 2

    Send that many tokens

    Move the same number of tokens to the other side.

  3. 3

    Clear your side to win

    First player with no tokens left wins!

Questions

Straight answers about DiceBack

The game is not out yet, so here is exactly where it stands today.

Will DiceBack be free?

Pricing is not final yet. The plan is a free download with optional cosmetic purchases only: token and dice skins, coin packs and bundles. Nothing in the store will change how a dice roll lands or how a match is won.

Can I play with a friend on one device?

Yes. That is exactly what 2 Players mode is for. Two people share a single phone and pass it back and forth, one roll each. No second device, no second account, no lobby to join.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play as a guest straight from the welcome screen. Signing in or creating an account is there so your matches, wins, streak, coins and level are saved instead of living only on that one device.

Is DiceBack kid-friendly?

Yes. The rules take about ten seconds to learn and read fine to a child. There is no gambling and no real-money wagering anywhere in the game. Dice and coins are game pieces, never a bet.

When does DiceBack launch?

It is still in development and we have not announced a release date. We would rather ship it when the matches feel right than name a date we have to walk back. Message us and we will tell you when it is live.

What platforms will it be on?

iOS and Android are planned for the first release. The store buttons on this page stay switched off until the game is genuinely downloadable, so if they are still gray, it is not out yet.

Want to hear when DiceBack launches?

No date is announced yet. Send us a line and we will email you the day it goes live on iOS and Android.
