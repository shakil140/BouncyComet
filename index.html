How we build

Four rules we do not break

Every idea that leaves the prototype folder has to clear the same four tests. If it fails one, it goes back or it goes away.

Learn it in ten seconds

Rules you can say out loud in one breath, and every action under a resting thumb. Each Dice Duo game explains itself in three short steps.

Fair by design

Both players sit down to the same rules and roll the same die. Every roll lands on the table where both of you can see it, and no match is ever played for real money.

Built to be replayed

Short matches, instant rematches and a daily reward for showing up. We tune for your tenth session, not your first minute.

Play first, sign in later

Open the app and play: against the computer or with a friend on the same phone, with no account and no connection. Sign in only when you want online matches.
