What we build

Six genres, one studio voice

Different shelves in the store, the same promise on every one of them: pick it up in seconds, feel good about the next round, never get squeezed for it.

🧩

Puzzle

We build puzzles you solve with a thumb and remember with your head. Honest logic, no hidden rules, and a board small enough to read at a glance.

⚡

Hypercasual

One input, one goal, understood before the first round ends. We prototype fast and keep only the loops that still feel good on the hundredth try.

🕹

Arcade

Short runs, rising tension, a score that dares you to beat it. Restart sits one tap away, because the next run is the whole point.

🔀

Hybrid-casual

Hypercasual feel with progression worth coming back for. Skins, streaks and daily goals layered onto a core loop that already stands on its own.

🤝

Multiplayer

Two players on one device, or a quick match against a stranger. Matches short enough for a bus ride and fair enough to ask for a rematch.

🧸

Kids & Family

Bright art, gentle pacing and readable buttons made for small hands. Games a parent can hand over without standing behind the screen.

How we build

Four rules we do not break

Every idea that leaves the prototype folder has to clear the same four tests. If it fails one, it goes back or it goes away.

👍

One-thumb playable

Every core action sits under a resting thumb on a phone held in one hand. If it needs two hands or a stretch to the far corner, we move it.

⏱

Learn it in ten seconds

Rules you can say out loud in one breath. DiceBack has exactly three, and the whole how to play screen fits on a single page with room to spare.

🛡

Fair by design

No pay to win and no traps in the store. Coins buy token skins, dice skins and style, never an advantage over the player sitting across from you.

🔁

Built to be replayed

Short matches, instant rematches, daily rewards and streaks that reward showing up. We tune for your tenth session, not your first minute.
