DiceBack - 2 Player Dice Game

Roll. Think. Bounce. Win! A head to head dice duel where small moves win big games, built for one quick round on the bus and ten more at home.

In development Multiplayer Board Casual
  • Three rules, one round to learn them. Roll the dice, send that many tokens to the other side, clear your side to win.
  • Three ways to play. VS Bot against a smart opponent, 2 Players sharing one device, or Quick Match against a random player.
  • Reasons to come back. A seven day reward ladder, token and dice skins, achievements and a weekly leaderboard.
  • No account needed. Jump in as a guest, or sign in to keep your matches, win streak and level progress.

In the lab

What we are prototyping next

None of these are announced games. They are the directions we build in, the kind of thing we are sketching and play testing right now. The moment one of them earns a name and a release date, it moves up this page.

In concept
🧩

Puzzle

Short, clever runs you can finish while the kettle boils. We are sketching boards where one simple rule generates a hundred different problems, plus a daily challenge worth opening the app for.

Prototype
⚡

Hypercasual

One input, one rule, understood in under three seconds. We build these fast and test them faster, and we only keep the loops that still feel good on the fiftieth attempt.

Prototype
🕹

Arcade

Thirty second rounds, a score that stings, and a retry button you hit before you have finished being annoyed. We are testing timing and reaction ideas with chunky feedback on every tap.

In concept
📈

Hybrid-casual

An arcade core wrapped in progression that gives you a reason to open the game tomorrow. We are working out how far a simple loop can stretch before it stops being simple.

In concept
🎈

Kids & Family

Bright, gentle games a small child can play without help and a parent can hand over without worrying. No pressure timers, no dark patterns, no shouting. Just something friendly to poke at.
