Featured title

2 Player Board Games

Pick a game. Challenge a friend.

One app, five two-player dice games. Every game is played with a single die and chunky toy pieces, and every one can be learned in a round.

  • Five games, one shelf. Grid War, DiceBack, Roll Race, Six Spots and Scoop Stack.
  • Four ways to play. Against the computer, two players on one phone, an online quick match, or a private room with a code.
  • Reasons to come back. A daily reward, skins for your dice and tokens, a profile and leaderboards.
  • No account needed to start. Play the computer or a friend on the same phone straight away, and sign in when you want online play.

Inside Dice Duo

Same die, five very different boards

A land grab, a tug of war, two races and a climb. Here is each one in a sentence, with the full rules a tap away.

Connect. Claim. Win.

Grid War

Roll the die and draw that many lines between the dots. Draw the fourth side of a box and it is yours.

Most boxes wins.

How to play Grid War

Send them back

DiceBack

Every roll sends that many of your tokens across the diagonal to the other side. What you send can come straight back.

First to empty their side wins.

How to play DiceBack

Roll. Mark. Race.

Roll Race

Roll a number and mark it on your card. Every number needs two marks, and a full row wastes the turn.

First full card wins.

How to play Roll Race

Match your roll

Six Spots

Roll and cover the matching spot on your triangle. A number you already own wastes the turn.

First to cover all six wins.

How to play Six Spots

Grow your total

Scoop Stack

Each roll stacks a new scoop carrying your running total. Eight scoops each, and a Cherry Roll settles a tie.

The higher total wins.

How to play Scoop Stack

In the lab

What we are prototyping next

None of these are announced games. They are the directions we build in, the kind of thing we are sketching and play testing right now. The moment one of them earns a name and a release date, it moves up this page.

In concept

Puzzle

Short, clever runs you can finish while the kettle boils. We are sketching boards where one simple rule generates a hundred different problems, plus a daily challenge worth opening the app for.

Prototype

Hypercasual

One input, one rule, understood in under three seconds. We build these fast and test them faster, and we only keep the loops that still feel good on the fiftieth attempt.

Prototype

Arcade

Thirty second rounds, a score that stings, and a retry button you hit before you have finished being annoyed. We are testing timing and reaction ideas with chunky feedback on every tap.

In concept

Hybrid-casual

An arcade core wrapped in progression that gives you a reason to open the game tomorrow. We are working out how far a simple loop can stretch before it stops being simple.

In concept

Kids & Family

Bright, gentle games a small child can play without help and a parent can hand over without worrying. No pressure timers, no dark patterns, no shouting. Just something friendly to poke at.
