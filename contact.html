Before you write

Three things worth knowing

A small studio answers its own mail. Here is what that means for you.

⌛

When you will hear back

Usually within a few business days. We are a small team with a game in development, so we will not pretend to a same-day guarantee we cannot keep.

If a week goes by with nothing, send it again. Mail does get lost, and a nudge is never rude.

📄

What to put in a publishing note

Tell us which titles you have published, the genres and regions you are strongest in, how you split revenue, and what you expect from a studio during soft launch.

Say whether you want to look at DiceBack or at something new. That one line saves a whole round of email.

🐞

How to report a DiceBack bug

Mail support@bouncycomet.com with your device and OS version, the game mode you were in, and what you expected to happen instead.

A screenshot or short screen recording of the moment it went wrong is worth more than any description. Send it and we can usually reproduce it the same day.
