1. Who we are and how to contact us

Bouncy Comet ("Bouncy Comet", "we", "us", "our") is an independent mobile game studio. We build puzzle, hypercasual, arcade, hybrid-casual, multiplayer and kids' games. We operate the website at bouncycomet.com and we develop and publish the games listed on it.

For anything to do with privacy, data protection or a request about your own data, contact us at studio@bouncycomet.com. That inbox is monitored by the people who actually run the studio, not by an outsourced ticket queue.

General enquiries, business questions and help with a game all reach the same place: studio@bouncycomet.com.

Bouncy Comet is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Where the law requires us to appoint a representative or a data protection officer and that obligation applies to us, we will name them in this policy rather than leave you guessing.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to two things:

  • This website at bouncycomet.com, including every page on it.
  • Our games, including DiceBack - 2 Player Dice Game, which is currently in development and has not launched yet.

It does not apply to services we do not control. If you follow a link from here to an app store, a social platform or any other third-party site, that company's own privacy policy governs what happens next. We cannot change their terms and we do not receive a copy of everything they collect.

Because DiceBack is still in development, some of the data handling described in section 4 describes the build as it currently works. If anything material changes before launch, we will update this policy and change the date at the top of the page before the game is released.

3. What we collect on this website

Almost nothing. This site is a set of static pages. There is no login, no account, no comment system and no shopping cart.

Server and CDN request logs

Like every website, ours is served by a hosting and content delivery provider that records standard technical request logs. Those logs typically include your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, the referring page and your browser's user agent string. We use them only to keep the site online, to investigate errors and abuse, and to understand rough traffic levels. We do not use them to build a profile of you.

Tracking, advertising and cookies

We do not run advertising trackers, retargeting pixels or third-party marketing scripts on this website. We do not sell or share website visitor data with data brokers.

We do not set cookies for tracking or advertising. If any browser storage is used on this site at all, it is strictly functional, for example remembering that you closed a notice, and it stays on your device.

We may add analytics later. If we do, we will choose a privacy-friendly option, we will say which one here, and we will update the date at the top of this page before it goes live. We will not quietly bolt on a tracker and leave this paragraph as it is.

Fonts and third-party assets

This site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google receives your IP address and standard request information as part of serving them. We do not receive anything back from that request and we do not use it to identify you.

When you email us

Our contact page opens your own email client rather than posting a form to a server we run. If you choose to write to us, we receive whatever you put in that email, including your email address, and we keep the correspondence so we can answer you and keep a record of the conversation.

4. What our games collect, including DiceBack

DiceBack can be played as a guest, without an account. What we hold depends on how you choose to play.

Guest play

If you play as a guest, your progress is stored on your device. Coins, level, unlocked token skins and dice skins and your match history stay local. If you delete the app or reset the device without signing in first, that progress is gone and we cannot restore it, because we never had a copy.

Account data, only if you create an account

If you choose to sign in or create an account so your progress is saved across devices, we hold an authentication identifier and the email address or sign-in identifier associated with it, plus the display name you pick. Choose a display name you are comfortable showing to other players, because it appears on leaderboards and in matches.

We never receive or store your password for a third-party sign-in provider. If you sign in through Apple or Google, that provider authenticates you and passes us an identifier, not your credentials.

Gameplay data

To save progress, run the Weekly Leaderboard, award achievements and match you fairly, we process gameplay data such as matches played, wins, best streak, coins, level and XP, daily reward claims, unlocked token skins and dice skins, and achievement progress including First Win, Win Streak, Token Master and Dice Collector. In online modes such as Quick Match, the basics needed to run the match, such as your display name and your moves, are visible to your opponent.

Device and diagnostic data

We process a device identifier and basic device and operating system information, such as device model, OS version and app version, for three narrow purposes: crash reporting and bug fixing, protecting the game against cheating and abuse, and making sure rewards such as the seven-day daily ladder are claimed once per player rather than farmed. We do not use this data for advertising profiles.

Purchases

In-game purchases, including coin packs, the Token Pack, the Dice Pack and the Starter Bundle, are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, not by us. Those platforms handle your payment method and send us a receipt or transaction confirmation so we can unlock what you bought. We never see or store your full card number, CVV or bank details. Refunds are handled under the store's own policy.

Settings

Your in-game settings, such as music, sound, vibration, notifications and language, are preferences stored so the game behaves the way you asked. If you turn notifications on, the platform issues a push token that lets us deliver them, and you can switch them off at any time in the game's settings or in your device settings.

5. Why we use this data

  • To run the game. Start matches, match you with an opponent, save progress, award daily rewards and keep the leaderboard accurate.
  • To support you. Answer emails to studio@bouncycomet.com, restore an account, or investigate a bug you reported.
  • To fix and improve things. Read crash reports, find the screens where players get stuck, and balance the game.
  • To keep play fair. Detect cheating, reward farming and abuse of online modes.
  • To meet legal obligations. Keep purchase and tax records where the law requires it, and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with data brokers. We do not use gameplay data to build advertising profiles.

6. Legal bases under the GDPR

If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or another region with similar rules, we rely on the following legal bases.

  • Performance of a contract. Running the game, saving your account progress, delivering what you bought and providing support. Without this data the service cannot work.
  • Legitimate interests. Keeping the service secure and free of cheating, fixing crashes, preventing fraud and abuse, and maintaining basic server logs. We balance these interests against your rights, and we keep the data narrow and short-lived.
  • Consent. Push notifications, and anything optional we add later such as analytics or marketing email. You can withdraw consent at any time, and withdrawing it does not affect processing that already happened.
  • Legal obligation. Retaining transaction and tax records, and complying with valid legal requests.

7. Third parties and service providers

We are deliberately conservative here, and we will not list a vendor we have not actually confirmed.

The third parties involved today are:

  • The Apple App Store and Google Play, for distributing our games and processing in-app purchases. Each operates under its own privacy policy and its own payment handling.
  • Our website hosting and content delivery provider, which serves bouncycomet.com and keeps the standard request logs described in section 3.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces used on this website.

DiceBack has not launched. Any backend, crash-reporting, analytics or advertising provider that is part of the live game at launch will be named in this policy before the game is released. We are not going to pretend today that we have finalised a stack we are still building.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, for example in response to a valid legal request, or where it is necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of players or of the studio. If Bouncy Comet is ever involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal data may transfer as part of that, and we will notify affected users and update this policy.

8. Children's privacy

We make games in genres that appeal to children, and we take that seriously.

  • We do not serve behaviourally targeted or interest-based advertising to children.
  • We do not sell children's personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We do not knowingly collect more personal data from a child than is needed to run the game.

Where the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) applies, we will not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent. Where the GDPR and its child-specific rules (often called GDPR-K) apply, we treat users below the applicable age of digital consent in their country accordingly, and we do not rely on consent from a child where the law requires a parent or guardian to give it.

We do not claim any third-party children's privacy certification, seal or safe harbour membership. If we ever obtain one, we will name it here.

Parents and guardians: if you believe a child has provided us with personal data, or you want that data reviewed or deleted, email studio@bouncycomet.com with the display name or account email used in the game. We will verify the request to the extent we reasonably can, then delete the data and close the account. We will not charge you for this and we will not make you jump through hoops.

Guest play on a shared family device keeps progress on that device. Deleting the app removes it.

9. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access. Ask what we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correction. Have inaccurate data fixed, including a wrong display name or email address.
  • Deletion. Ask us to erase your data, subject to records we are legally required to keep.
  • Portability. Receive the data you gave us in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Objection and restriction. Object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interests, or ask us to restrict it while a dispute is resolved.
  • Withdrawal of consent. Turn off anything you opted into, such as push notifications, at any time.

To use any of these rights, email studio@bouncycomet.com from the address on the account, or tell us the display name and platform you play on so we can find the right record. We will respond within one month, and we will tell you if we need longer because the request is complex. We do not charge for this. We may ask for enough information to be sure the request really comes from you, because handing an account over to the wrong person is its own privacy failure.

If you think we have handled your data badly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection or privacy supervisory authority, and you do not need our permission to do so.

10. How long we keep data, and deletion

  • Account and gameplay data is kept while your account is active, so your progress, coins, skins and streaks are there when you come back.
  • Inactive accounts. If an account goes unused for a long period, we may delete or anonymise the data associated with it rather than hold it indefinitely.
  • Crash and diagnostic data is kept only as long as it is useful for fixing the problem, then deleted or aggregated so it is no longer linked to a device.
  • Website request logs are kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting, then rotated out.
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve the issue and keep a reasonable record of it.

You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing studio@bouncycomet.com. Deletion removes your account data and gameplay progress, and it cannot be undone. Anonymised or aggregated statistics that can no longer identify you, and records we must legally retain such as purchase receipts, may remain.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data: encrypted connections for data in transit, access limited to the people who need it, and third-party platforms chosen for handling authentication and payments rather than rolling our own.

No online service can promise perfect security, and we are not going to claim otherwise. If a breach affects your personal data and the law requires us to notify you, we will, without hiding behind vague wording about an "incident".

12. International transfers

We are a small studio and the services we rely on, including app stores and hosting providers, operate globally. That means your data may be processed in a country other than your own, including outside the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area, where data protection law may differ.

Where we transfer personal data internationally, we rely on an appropriate safeguard recognised under applicable law, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision, and we expect the same of the providers we use.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when what we do changes, and specifically before DiceBack launches, once the live service stack is confirmed. When we update it we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

If a change materially affects how we handle your personal data, we will do more than change a date: we will give notice in the game, on this website, or by email where we have your address and the change warrants it. Where the law requires fresh consent for a change, we will ask for it rather than assume it.

Questions about any of this go to studio@bouncycomet.com. A real person reads it.
