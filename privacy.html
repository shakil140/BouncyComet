1. Studio and contact

Bouncy Comet ("we", "us") develops and operates Dice Duo and bouncycomet.com. We are responsible for the account and game-service data we process. Advertising and other providers may also process data for their own purposes, as explained below.

For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, email privacy@bouncycomet.com. For game help use support@bouncycomet.com. Legal enquiries go to legal@bouncycomet.com.

2. Scope

This policy describes the website and the current Android Dice Duo service, including offline bot play, local two-player play and online games. DiceBack, Six Spots and Scoop Stack are games inside Dice Duo. Future games or materially different features may need an updated policy. Links to another company's site, app store or advertisement are subject to that company's own policies.

3. Website and email

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its hosting and delivery services receive technical request information such as IP address, requested page, browser information and time. The site has no player login, payment form or advertising tracker. Its contact and deletion-request buttons open your email application; they do not submit a web form to our game server.

Some pages load Google Fonts. Google receives IP address and normal browser request information when serving those fonts. The account-deletion page uses system fonts and does not require a third-party font request.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Our studio mailbox and game verification messages use Google's email services. We use correspondence for support, handling privacy requests and keeping necessary records of those requests. Do not send us passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

4. Dice Duo data

Offline play and local settings

Offline bot and same-device two-player games can be played without sign-in. Local saves, game choices, sound and vibration settings remain on your device unless a feature sends them to the service as described below. Your Android or device backup settings may affect whether local files are backed up or restored. Signing out, deleting a server account and uninstalling the app are different actions; none should be assumed to erase device-provider backups.

Playing without an account does not mean no network or advertising data can be processed. The app retrieves service configuration and advertising components can process technical data when they operate.

Accounts and authentication

For an email account we process your email address, chosen display name, account ID, a securely hashed password, verification/reset records and login sessions. A password entered into the app is sent to our server over HTTPS for authentication; the account database stores a password hash rather than the original password.

For Google sign-in we receive the sign-in identifier and account information Google provides, such as email, verification status and a name used to initialise your game profile. We do not receive your Google password. Online matches, private rooms, the online store, daily rewards and leaderboards require sign-in and a connection.

Gameplay and public profile

The online service processes match participation, moves and results, activity times, rating, level and XP, virtual-coin balances and transactions, reward claims and cosmetic inventory. Offline results may be reported when you use signed-in reward or progress features. These records operate matches, progress, rewards and leaderboards and help investigate cheating or disputed rewards.

Your display name, selected game avatar and relevant game statistics can appear to opponents and on leaderboards. Choose a nickname that does not reveal private information. Avatars and skins are selected from game-provided artwork; the current game does not offer player photo uploads or player-to-player chat.

Device, session and service records

We process installation/device identifiers, device name, IP address, browser or client information, app/service activity times and authentication tokens. Service, security and advertising-reward records may include account IDs and technical request details. These support authentication, connectivity, fraud prevention, troubleshooting and correct rewards. IP address may also be used by advertising providers to infer approximate location; the game does not request precise GPS location.

Advertising and measurement

Dice Duo includes Unity LevelPlay / ironSource mediation, Unity Ads, Google's Mobile Ads components and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ads may be switched on or off by service configuration. When available, rewarded ads are optional and can offer virtual coins. Other supported ad formats can be displayed under the applicable game settings. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not disable every SDK's technical processing.

Advertising providers can collect and share advertising and installation/device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, account-related identifiers, ad views and interactions, device/app information and performance diagnostics. Unity Ads' data disclosure also lists purchase history. The game itself records virtual-coin cosmetic transactions; these disclosures do not mean that Dice Duo takes cash payments. Providers use data to deliver and measure ads, analyse performance, prevent fraud and operate their services. Depending on your choices and the applicable provider rules, this can include personalised advertising. A mediation provider may send requests to its enabled advertising partners.

The current build does not collect a player's age or date of birth. Because consent age is unknown, its ad requests use conservative under-age and child-treatment restrictions, advertising-ID opt-out and no-sale/sharing treatment. Ads remain disabled if these request restrictions cannot be applied. These restrictions can reduce available ads and do not stop every SDK's technical processing, such as IP address, device information and diagnostics. They do not change the game's intended audience of players aged 13 and over.

The app uses UMP to request applicable advertising privacy choices before its explicit ad-provider initialisation. Privacy choices and available forms depend on region, under-age treatment and provider settings. Opening the game's Privacy/Ad choices option pauses ad requests and discards loaded ads; further requests depend on current permission and successful privacy handling. Use this option when available, your Android advertising controls, or contact us for help. Resetting an advertising ID does not remove every installation or account identifier. Account deletion is not the same as withdrawing an advertising provider's consent.

The current game does not take card or bank details and does not implement cash purchases, cash prizes, withdrawals or real-money wagering. Virtual-coin spending and cosmetic transaction records are game data, not a bank balance.

5. Uses of data

  • Game functionality and account management: verify accounts, authenticate sessions, run online matches, save supported progress, manage cosmetics and award virtual rewards.
  • Security and fair play: detect misuse, investigate invalid rewards, enforce bans and protect accounts and the service.
  • Diagnostics and analytics: investigate service faults and measure advertising and SDK performance.
  • Advertising: request, deliver, personalise where permitted and measure advertisements.
  • Communications and legal duties: send verification, reset and account messages, answer requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell an email list or provide account passwords to advertising partners. Advertising-related sharing may be treated as a sale or sharing under some privacy laws even without a payment for the data. The provider disclosures and your applicable privacy choices matter; this policy does not claim that advertising sharing never occurs.

6. Legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, account and requested gameplay processing supports the service you request; security, fraud prevention and necessary operations rely on legitimate interests where allowed; consent applies to optional advertising/device processing where required; and legal obligations apply to records or disclosures required by law. You may withdraw consent or object as applicable. We do not treat reading this policy as consent to every kind of processing.

7. Providers and sharing

  • Google: Google Play distribution, Google sign-in, Gmail-based transactional/studio email, Google Fonts on the website, Mobile Ads and UMP. See Google's Privacy Policy and advertising information.
  • Unity / ironSource: LevelPlay mediation and Unity Ads, including enabled advertising partners. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and LevelPlay legal resources.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting and delivery. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Game-server infrastructure: hosting, storage and network services used to operate our API and databases. These can process technical/account data as service providers. Write to our privacy contact for questions about your records and the relevant infrastructure.

Other players can see the public game profile and gameplay described above. Authorised studio staff can access records needed for support, moderation, security and operations. We may disclose relevant records for valid legal requirements or to protect rights and safety. Advertising providers may have independent purposes and retention practices; their processing is not limited to instructions from us.

8. Age and parental requests

Dice Duo is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not intended for children under 13. Being 13 does not automatically make someone an adult or old enough to give privacy consent in every country. Players must meet local eligibility rules and obtain parent or guardian permission where required. A store age rating describes content and is not a substitute for these requirements.

If you believe a child below the permitted age has provided personal data, or you are a parent or guardian making a request, contact privacy@bouncycomet.com. We will take reasonable steps to verify the request and remove the affected account/data as applicable, subject to necessary security or legal retention. We do not claim a children's privacy certification or that the current app includes a verified parental-consent system.

9. Choices and rights

You can play supported offline modes without a player account, change supported profile/settings options, use available advertising privacy choices and request account deletion. Depending on the law where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete or receive your data, restrict processing, object, withdraw consent or complain to a privacy regulator.

Email privacy@bouncycomet.com to make a request. Include the account email and the request; do not include your password. We may need to verify ownership or parental authority before acting. We respond within applicable legal deadlines; if a lawful extension is needed we will explain it. Requests about a provider's independent records may also need that provider's own privacy process.

10. Retention and account deletion

Delete in the game: Profile → Account → Delete account. Complete the confirmation and ownership check. Request deletion without installing or signing into the game: use our Dice Duo account-deletion page or email privacy@bouncycomet.com. Email requests are handled by studio staff after ownership verification.

Deletion revokes player sessions and removes the live account, profile/progress, balances, coin ledger, inventory, personal login links and associated account authentication records. Public match display names are changed to "Deleted player". Deletion is permanent. An account deletion does not erase another player's record of a match or automatically delete your device's local files or provider-managed backups.

  • Active account/progress: retained while the account exists. Unverified sign-ups are eligible for cleanup after 7 days.
  • Routine authentication/email records: verification/reset records are eligible for cleanup after 2 days; transactional email outbox records after 30 days; expired refresh-session records 7 days after expiry. Cleanup is periodic, so these are cleanup thresholds rather than exact deletion times.
  • Administrative/security audit records: current routine cleanup threshold is 730 days.
  • Advertising reward callbacks: current routine cleanup threshold is 90 days. Account links are detached where supported; callback payloads can contain technical details or a previous account identifier until cleanup.
  • Remaining match history: match/player identifiers and results can remain after deletion for opponent history and service integrity, with the public name replaced. An identifier alone is not a promise of full anonymisation.
  • Security exceptions: bans, staff moderation notes and blocked-identity hashes can remain to investigate abuse and prevent ban evasion. Historical configuration can also contain old player IDs.
  • Backups and operational logs: may contain older records after live deletion and are access-restricted operational copies. Current match/security exceptions, historical configuration, backup files and operational logs do not all have an automatic expiry. We therefore cannot promise that every copy or identifier disappears after a fixed number of days. Applicable rights and necessary-retention reviews still apply.
  • Correspondence and provider data: support/privacy correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. Advertising, email and hosting providers apply their own retention rules to records they independently control.

The deletion page gives the steps, ownership checks and retained-data details. Contact us if you need an explanation of retained information about your account.

11. Security

The app's public account/game-server connections use HTTPS and secure WebSockets. Transactional mail uses an encrypted SMTP connection. The listed advertising SDK providers document TLS for their data transfers. We hash account passwords, limit administrative access and revoke sessions during deletion. These measures do not mean data is end-to-end encrypted or that every stored file/database/backup is encrypted at rest.

No system can guarantee perfect security. If a personal-data breach requires notification under applicable law, we will provide the required notice.

12. International processing

Our hosting, Google, GitHub and advertising providers can process data in countries other than yours, with different privacy rules. Their policies describe their international processing and safeguards. Where applicable law requires additional safeguards for our transfers, those requirements apply; this policy does not claim that every provider contract or transfer arrangement has been independently certified.

13. Updates

We update this policy when the service or its data practices materially change and update the date above. Where required, we provide additional notice or seek new consent. Questions go to privacy@bouncycomet.com.
